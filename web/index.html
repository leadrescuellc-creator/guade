BANK ACCOUNTSTOOLS

Use a bank or financial-data provider that supports an authorization flow. GUADE does not collect bank passwords, routing numbers, or full account numbers.

Configure banking connector →
CRYPTO WALLETSTOOLS

Connect wallet software through a trusted provider for supported public-address or signing tools. Never enter a seed phrase or private key into GUADE, chat, or an agent task.

Configure wallet connector →
BUSINESS IDENTITYEIN / D-U-N-S

Use an authorized tax or business-identity provider connector for EIN and D-U-N-S workflows. These identifiers are sensitive; GUADE does not store them in connector settings.

Configure identity connector →

These are connector slots, not direct bank, wallet, tax, or payment processor integrations. Only connect providers you trust, inspect their tools, and grant the narrowest access needed. GUADE does not initiate payments or custody funds by itself.
